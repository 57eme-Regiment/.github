## What is happening here?

The previous generation of projects has been **archived**. Everything is preserved,
but nothing is maintained, and no new work will land in those repositories.
The archived projects are now **private** — visible to organisation members only.

> **Nothing in this organisation is currently under active development.**
> The new generation is being planned and will appear here as it becomes ready.

*FR — Refonte en cours. Les anciens projets sont archivés en lecture seule et réservés
aux membres de l'organisation ; la nouvelle génération est en préparation.*
